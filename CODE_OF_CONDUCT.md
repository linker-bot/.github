# Code of conduct

We want Linkerbot's open-source spaces to be welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, and other community spaces maintained by the organization.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas, code, and evidence.
- Respect privacy and acknowledge others' work.
- Make room for newcomers and people writing in a second language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at participants, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable. Spam and repeated disruption of project discussions are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns privately to [helloworld@linkerbot.cn](mailto:helloworld@linkerbot.cn), the organization's published contact address. Include links and enough context to understand the incident; avoid distributing private information publicly. If the concern involves a maintainer, explain that in the report.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change their behavior, restrict participation, or block participants according to the severity and repetition of the conduct. Reports should be handled with care for the privacy of those involved; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
